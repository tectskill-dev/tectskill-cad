TectSkill CAD
==========================================

TectSkill CAD is a modified version of CADCraft by the ArtCraft team
(https://github.com/storytold/cadcraft), based on source revision
336243e0afb3019feee58d0d4fc0d218f25530e7.

Changes made by TectSkill:
  - Thai text support: automatic Thai fallback fonts, and Thai shown in the
    interface and the text editor.
  - FONTALT and FONTFALLBACK are kept between sessions.
  - Product name, About window and icon changed to TectSkill CAD.
  - Automatic updates to CADCraft turned off; Help > Check for Updates opens
    the TectSkill CAD download page.
  - ArtCraft name, logos and links removed, as CADCraft's brand terms require.
