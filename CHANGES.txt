TectSkill CAD
==========================================

TectSkill CAD is a modified version of CADCraft by the ArtCraft team
(https://github.com/storytold/cadcraft). This release is based on CADCraft v0.18.0
(source revision 32238e83c2c8bf643994132883e94bc96961f649), and its version number
follows CADCraft's, with TectSkill's revision added as a fourth number
(0.18.0.2 is TectSkill's second revision on CADCraft 0.18.0).

Thai text in drawings (Thai fonts, stacked vowels and tone marks, MTEXT word wrap)
comes from CADCraft itself.

Changes made by TectSkill:
  - Thai interface: every menu, dialog and prompt translated to Thai (th.tsv),
    Thai is the starting language, and Options > Interface language switches
    to English or any other language. Command names stay in English.
  - Thai shown in the interface: the in-place text editor, Properties panel,
    tabs and layer names.
  - FONTALT and FONTFALLBACK are kept between sessions.
  - Automatic updates to CADCraft turned off; Help > Check for Updates opens
    the TectSkill CAD download page.
  - Product name, About window and icon changed to TectSkill CAD.
  - ArtCraft name, logos and links removed, as CADCraft's brand terms require.
